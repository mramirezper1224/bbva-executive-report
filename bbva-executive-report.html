Definitions, sources & reporting notes

Metric definitions: RoTE = net attributable profit / average tangible equity. CET1 ratio = common equity tier 1 capital / risk-weighted assets. Cost-to-income = operating expenses / total revenue. These are simplified template definitions; align with each issuer’s disclosures.
